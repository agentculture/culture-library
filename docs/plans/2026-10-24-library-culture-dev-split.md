### t5 — Keys with scopes and Cloudflare Access verification

- instruction: Cite xteink core/keys.py and server/{auth,access}.py (ea2f643); pocketsync web/access.py is the stdlib reference for the JWT verifier. Files: `culture_library`/core/keys.py, `culture_library`/server/{auth,access}.py, tests/server/`test_auth.py`.
- covers: c6, h6
- acceptance:
  - `culture_library`/core/keys.py mints hash-only keys with scopes read, write, admin and an optional kind list; server/auth.py enforces them: read key gets 403 on POST/DELETE and /api/keys; a write key scoped to diary gets 403 on other kinds

### t6 — Change feed

- covers: c34, h24
- acceptance:
  - `culture_library`/core/feed.py appends created, updated, archived, purged and `send_requested` events with item id, sha256 and a monotonic cursor; GET /api/feed?since=`<cursor>` returns events in order
  - tests/core/`test_feed.py`: after a mix of creates, replaces and deletes, a reader from a saved cursor sees exactly those events with no catalog scan

### t7 — Search: full-text and semantic with a background indexer

- instruction: Read the gateway URL and key from env (never written to disk). Measure embedding cost on the real corpus and record it in the task's evidence. Files: `culture_library`/core/{search,indexer}.py, tests/core/`test_search.py`.
- covers: c41, h31
- acceptance:

  - `culture_library`/core/indexer.py indexes in the background with retries; with the gateway stopped an upload still returns 201 and is title-searchable, and full-text/semantic catch up later
  - index lag, failures and last success are exposed as data for the status endpoint

## End state

### Announcement

- library.culture.dev is Culture's one library: pipelines publish recordings, EPUB books, PDFs, newspapers and Markdown into it; it also hosts every repo's devague record — specs, plans and .devague content; Gwen (embodiment) reads, cites and remembers from it and keeps her diary there; it replaces ebooks.culture.dev, and xteink becomes a device-side client of it

### After state

- Opening library.culture.dev shows one catalog — books, newspapers, documents, PDFs, transcripts, Gwen's diary and every repo's specs, plans and devague records — grouped by day with kind filters, full-text and semantic search, and a reader for each kind; culture-rules pushes the daily newspaper and transcripts into it, xteink pulls from it, Gwen asks it questions over MCP and writes her diary, and ebooks.culture.dev is gone
